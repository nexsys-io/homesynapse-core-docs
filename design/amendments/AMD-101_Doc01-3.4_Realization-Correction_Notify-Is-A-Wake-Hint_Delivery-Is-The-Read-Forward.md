# AMD-101 — Doc 01 §3.4 realization correction: the notification is a wake hint; delivery is the read-forward

## §1 Rationale
Doc 01 §3.4 describes the subscriber lifecycle as: the bus unparks the subscriber when matching events exist beyond its checkpoint (step 3); the subscriber polls `readFrom(lastCheckpoint, batchSize, filter)` (step 4); it processes each event, then acknowledges by writing its checkpoint (step 5). In that model the notification carries no position; the store is the only source of what is delivered and in what order.

The realization (`InProcessEventBus`, M3.x through FIX-2b-ii) delivers something else. `notifyEvent(P)` runs on the publisher's thread after each append (`NotifyingEventPublisher`, lifecycle, with no lock across append and notify), reads the subscriber's PERSISTED checkpoint, skips when `checkpoint >= P`, and otherwise offers `P` itself into the subscriber's pending queue; the LIVE loop polls that queue and delivers the positions it is handed, in the order they arrived, writing each delivered position as the new checkpoint through an `INSERT OR REPLACE` upsert. Three consequences follow, all measured or proven at source in September 2026:

1. **Delivery order follows notification order.** Two publishers whose notifies interleave deliver `[P+1, P]`; the persisted checkpoint then regresses from `P+1` to `P` (the upsert has no monotonic guard). Both positions were delivered, but the checkpoint no longer says so. TR-1's position census (`delivered := position ≤ checkpoint`) reports `missed=1 first_missed=P+1` — the exact tuple of ci samples #11 and #12.
2. **A late notification is dropped for good.** If `P+1` is delivered before `P`'s notify arrives, the guard `checkpoint >= P` skips `P`; nothing re-reads it. The census counts `P` as delivered (it is below the checkpoint). FIX-2b-ii (i) (core `72efb42`) made that skip visible as `DeliveryAnomaly.Kind.NOTIFY_SKIPPED_LIVE`; nothing prevents it.
3. **The checkpoint carries two meanings** — the durable restart floor and a delivery guard — and the second is the one that fails.

The design already names the correct model. The amendment states its invariant once, so the realization can be tested against it, and rules the parts of the realization that must change.

## §2 The invariant (the sentence BUS-ORDER-1 is written to; Nick's acceptance bar, 2026-09-12)
**Every position whose event matches a LIVE subscriber's filter is delivered to that subscriber exactly once, in position order, regardless of the order in which notifications arrive.**

Corollaries that the realization must hold:
- **The read-forward cursor is the source of truth for delivery.** Each active subscriber holds an in-memory cursor `maxDelivered` (the highest position delivered or filtered past in this activation); LIVE delivery reads the store forward from `maxDelivered` in pages and delivers each matching envelope in order. The cursor advances only forward.
- **The persisted checkpoint is the durable floor for restart, never a delivery guard.** It is written after delivery (per delivery for non-atomic subscribers; on the projection's own cadence for the atomic one — AMD-45 §2.2), it is monotonic by construction (it follows the cursor), and no path reads it to decide whether to offer, wake or deliver a position.
- **The notification is a wake hint plus a bounded idle tick.** `notifyEvent(P)` wakes the subscriber's thread; it carries no delivery decision and is never skipped for a LIVE subscriber (a wake with nothing new beyond the cursor costs one empty page read). A lost wake costs at most one idle tick before the next page read, never a stalled run.
- **Exactly once across the mode flip.** REPLAY pages to the head from the persisted checkpoint; the TRANSITION → LIVE flip hands the cursor, not a queue: the first LIVE page starts at the replay's last delivered position, so a position appended during the flip is delivered once, by whichever phase reaches it first, and never twice.
- **The store's append order is the delivery order.** The single write thread commits positions in sequence, so when `P+1` is readable, `P` is readable; a page never has a hole. A position not yet visible to a reader ends the page early and is read by the next wake or tick (this retires the per-position retry-and-suspend of FIX-1b on the LIVE path; the anomaly kinds it minted stay defined, per INV-GA-02, and stop being emitted by the LIVE path).

## §3 What changes in the realization (BUS-ORDER-1; nexsys-hivemind context/instructions/2026-09-12_coder-lane_BUS-ORDER-1_…_coding-instruction.md carries the mechanics)
1. `SubscriberRuntime` gains the cursor (`maxDelivered`, an `AtomicLong` initialised from the persisted checkpoint at activation) and a wake signal; the pending-positions queue becomes the wake signal's carrier (its contents are hints, not the unit of delivery; `pendingDepth` in `SubscriberSnapshot` reports un-consumed hints).
2. `liveLoop` becomes: wake (a hint or the idle tick) → page `readFrom(maxDelivered, batch)` → for each envelope in order: filter → deliver through the existing supervisor (DLQ, circuit breaker, SUSPENDED semantics unchanged) → advance the cursor → checkpoint (non-atomic subscribers) → repeat until an empty page → park with the idle tick.
3. `notifyEvent` and `offerUnfiltered`: for a LIVE subscriber, wake unconditionally; the persisted-checkpoint guard is removed from the LIVE path (it stays for passive registrations, which are callbacks, not loops). `NOTIFY_SKIPPED_LIVE` stops being reachable and stays defined as the tripwire's name.
4. `TransitionCoordinator`: the flip hands the cursor; the replay-window queue's drained positions are delivered by the read-forward from the cursor, not re-offered; `onCaughtUp` semantics (AMD-42 §3.4.3) unchanged.
5. `EventBusConfig` gains `liveReadBatch` (default 64) and `liveIdleTick` (default 250 ms) — Doc 01 §9 gains the two rows.
6. Tests, red-first in `core/event-bus`: (T1) two publishers notifying out of order deliver both, in order, once, and the persisted checkpoint never regresses; (T2) the TRANSITION → LIVE flip with appends during the drain delivers each position exactly once; (T3) a suppressed wake delivers within one idle tick. `ReplayTransitionIT` and the lifecycle ITs are untouched and green; the soak at K=200 under two carriers green on the desk; then twenty consecutive `bus-soak` samples green with kind E at zero (the closure of OR-BUS-SILENT-DROP).

## §4 The Doc 01 §3.4 edit (executes at ratification)
After the "Subscriber lifecycle" list (step 6), insert:

> **Delivery invariant (AMD-101, 2026-09-12).** Every position whose event matches a LIVE subscriber's filter is delivered to that subscriber exactly once, in position order, regardless of the order in which notifications arrive. The notification is a wake hint; it carries no position to deliver and is never a reason to skip one. Delivery is the read-forward from the subscriber's in-memory cursor (the highest position delivered in this activation), in pages, in store order; a bounded idle tick bounds the cost of a lost wake to one empty page read. The persisted checkpoint is the durable floor for restart, written after delivery and monotonic; no delivery decision reads it. Realized by BUS-ORDER-1 (core, September 2026); the drifted realization it replaces (notification-ordered delivery through a per-subscriber pending queue with a persisted-checkpoint guard) is described in AMD-101 §1.

And in §9 (Configuration), two rows: `liveReadBatch` (events per LIVE page read; default 64) · `liveIdleTick` (the LIVE loop's bounded park; default 250 ms).

## §5 The DAS consistency check

**Scope and consistency checks at ratification (v72 beat 4).** INV-PROJ-04's statement names "a subscriber's persisted checkpoint" although its category preamble is the State Projection's discipline: subscriber checkpoints are in scope by the invariant's own words, and the pre-AMD-101 LIVE path violated it (`P+1` written before `P` under out-of-order notifications), so the clause was placed there. Doc 01 §11 lists no anomaly kinds, so §11 is not edited. Kinds emitted from `core/event-bus` `src/main` at `a458a64`: `LIVE_READ_FAILED` (once per failed page read, `InProcessEventBus:574`) and `NOTIFY_NOT_VISIBLE` (the FIX-1a fan-out kind on the notify path, `:337`); `LIVE_READ_EMPTY`, `LIVE_READ_EXHAUSTED`, `NOTIFY_SKIPPED_LIVE`, `TRANSITION_READ_EMPTY` and `TRANSITION_READ_EXHAUSTED` are defined (INV-GA-02) and unemitted — the soak's `anomalies=0` therefore covers the two kinds that can still fire. This amendment says nothing about the run-hand-off face of the soak class; FIX-2b-ii's four tokens stand and that question belongs to OR-BUS-SILENT-DROP.
The amendment adds one invariant sentence and one paragraph in the register Doc 01 already uses (mechanism beside every quality word; no antithesis; the Glossary's terms: Subscriber, Checkpoint, Position, Event Store). No Glossary entry changes; no API token changes; no event payload changes.
